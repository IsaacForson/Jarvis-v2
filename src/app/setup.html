Set up Jarvis

Go down the list and press each button. macOS asks you to confirm each permission itself; no app can switch them on alone. Each step ticks itself once it is allowed.

  1. 1
    API keysYour NVIDIA keys (and Groq keys, optional), separated by commas. Kept in your Keychain.
  2. 2
    MicrophoneTo hear you. Press Allow in the box macOS shows.
  3. 3
    AccessibilityTo use apps for you: click, type, menus. In the settings that open, switch Jarvis on.
  4. 4
    Screen RecordingTo look at the screen, take screenshots and recordings. Switch Jarvis on; macOS may ask to reopen it.
  5. 5
    Calendar, Mail and System EventsTo read your calendar and mail. Press OK in each box macOS shows.
  6. 6
    Camera optionalOnly for hand control and "look at this". Off until you ask.
  7. 7
    Google Chrome optionalJarvis browses the web in its own Chrome window.
