Jarvis

Jarvis is a voice assistant for your Mac: say "Jarvis" or "Friday" and ask. It answers out loud, works in your apps, reads your calendar and mail, browses the web and remembers what you tell it.

This puts Jarvis in your Applications folder (about 2 GB, mostly its speech models, so listening stays on this Mac).

When it is installed, Jarvis opens a short setup window. macOS lets no app switch on its own microphone, accessibility or screen recording, so setup brings up each of macOS's own questions in turn: one click each.

You need your NVIDIA API keys (and, optionally, Groq keys) for Jarvis to think. Google Chrome is needed for web tasks.
